GRADEA — THE FREQUENCY BEAT PLAYER

Includes original uploaded beats, including MP3 conversions of two WAVs for smaller web files.

Install: Upload index.html, style.css, script.js and beat-player.js to the ROOT of the GitHub Pages repository. Upload assets/beats/track-01.mp3 through track-08.mp3 into assets/beats/ (create the folder first). Keep all existing assets including skyline.mp4 and skyline.jpg. Do not delete existing site assets.

The Instagram embeds from your most recent site are preserved. The album catalog remains powered by your existing script.js. The new player does not autoplay until the visitor clicks play. Playback continues while scrolling within the same page, but stops on page reload/navigation.

Order: B-APlaya, BirdzSing, RockWitYa, OnThatFR, amtrakk, Redy$U, EvryThingEyeNeed, HarpHeart.

IMPORTANT: These MP3s are publicly accessible to anyone with the file URL. Do not upload tracks you do not want publicly shared.
