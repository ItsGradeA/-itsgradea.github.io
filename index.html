ABOUT GRADEA.

North Carolina roots. A sound of its own.

GradeA is a North Carolina-based music producer blending soulful samples, Southern hip-hop influences, and laid-back rhythms into a sound of his own.

Inspired by everyday experiences, different places, and the music that shaped him, GradeA creates instrumentals that bring together classic sounds and modern production.

From beat tapes to live sessions, it's all about creating something you can feel.
